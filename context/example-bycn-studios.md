Version 0.4 · 9 November 2026 · Owner: Camille

Every agent reads this file before it does anything. Change the business here once, and every agent follows.

## 1. The business

- **Name:** BYCN.studios
- **Website:** bycamillenicole.com
- **What it does:** Digital marketing with AI for founder-led brands. That covers strategy, content, AI automation, SEO and GEO, and small websites.
- **Run by:** Camille Nicole, a freelance digital marketer and content creator.
- **Based in:** Oslo, Norway. Works remotely in English.

## 2. Ideal client

- **Who:** Solopreneurs and founder-led brands with 1 to 50 people, where the founder still makes the marketing decisions. Most clients are solo founders or very small teams.
- **Strong fit, first priority:** B2B tech and AI startups, including robotics, biotech and SaaS, that need content, automation and a clear story for technical products.
- **Also a good fit:** Small consumer brands launching or relaunching a website or social media.
- **Where:** Norway and Switzerland first, then anywhere in Europe working in English.
- **Not a fit:**
  - Large companies that want a full agency team.
  - Requests for free work.
  - Event planning or organising, since BYCN.studios only offers event photography, event materials and event campaigns.
  - Anything outside marketing, such as accounting or IT support.

## 5. Brand voice

- Natural human voice, clear and straightforward, like a real person talking.
- Friendly and professional, and a little fun.
- Business pages stay neutral about team size. Blog posts and stories can be first person.
- **Never use:**
  - Em dashes.
  - Flowery or poetic wording.
  - Overused AI phrases such as "let's dive in", "game-changer", "elevate" or "unlock".
  - Choppy, punchy fragment sentences.
- Explain technical things in simple, beginner-friendly words.

## 9. Lead scoring guide (used by the Lead agent)

Start at 0 and add or subtract points.

| Signal | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Decision maker (founder, co-founder, owner, CEO, CTO, head of marketing or marketing lead) | +25 |
| Solo founder or company with 1 to 50 people (count it as founder-led unless the lead says otherwise) | +20 |
| Asks for a listed service | +20 |
| Mentions a budget, timeline or start date | +15 |
| B2B tech, AI, robotics or biotech company | +10 |
| Based in Norway, Switzerland or elsewhere in Europe | +10 |
| Company has over 200 people | -20 |
| Student, job seeker or wants free help | -30 |
| Asks for something BYCN.studios doesn't offer | -30 |
| Spam, unrelated sales pitch or link-dropping | Score 0, no reply |

The highest score is 100. A real person who scores below 1 counts as cold (score 1), not spam.

**Score bands**

| Score | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 70 and above (hot) | Alert Camille now, with a reply draft offering a short call |
| 40 to 69 (warm) | Reply draft with the right package and one question |
| 1 to 39 (cold) | Polite reply draft, with a pointer to the blog or the Tingi check-up |
| 0 (spam) | Tag it as spam, no reply |
